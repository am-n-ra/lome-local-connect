# Omni V2 UI audit — canonical domain — 2026-08-22

## Evidence captured

The four-width proof ran against `https://omni.sparkafrika.online`. At 320 px, the live map, search, rail and sheets rendered; at 375, 768 and 1280 px the fixed 8.5-second wait sometimes ended while discovery was still loading, even though no console/page errors were recorded. The 320 and 1280 screenshots were inspected visually.

## Concrete layout findings

At 320 px the facility result rail sits too close to the bottom dock and visibly intersects its upper edge. The dock status line and the map attribution also occupy the same bottom band and visually collide. The bottom composition therefore does not satisfy the no-overlap rule even though document width equals viewport width.

At 1280 px the composition is substantially cleaner: the globe remains dominant and centered, the search pill is centered, the left map controls are separate, and the rail is absent while the page is still loading. The desktop screenshot did not show an obvious collision, but the proof must wait on the discovery state before judging the desktop rail and detail sheet.

## Nature Way correction targets

1. Make bottom overlays use a single layout budget: dock, status, attribution, map status, controls and result rail must have non-overlapping safe-area positions.
2. Keep the rail above the dock by a measured gap at mobile widths, including safe-area insets.
3. Move the attribution into a non-competing corner or reserve a dedicated band so it cannot share the dock-status baseline.
4. Replace the fixed-only proof wait with state-based waiting for either discovery completion or an explicit error/empty state, then assert no overlap at all four widths.
5. Preserve the map-first hierarchy, restrained glass surfaces, visible focus states, reduced-motion behavior and the current public/unclaimed trust language.

## Scope boundary

This audit concerns the buyer Trunk only. It does not redesign seller, QR, wallet or payment surfaces, and it does not change Neon Auth identities or historical database tables.
