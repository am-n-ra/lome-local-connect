# Sidereal Reflection Ledger Reference

Use this reference to create a private, non-deterministic reflection record. It is a personal practice artifact, not a prediction engine, personnel record, investment recommendation, or substitute for professional judgment.

## Opening record

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Framework | Vedic sidereal / Lahiri ayanamsha / whole-sign houses, unless user explicitly selects another system. |
| Scope and consent | What the user wants to explore and whether personal chart data may be used for this session only. |
| Chart basis | User-supplied chart or named calculation source; known uncertainty in birth data/time zone. |
| Structural path | Product / feature / flow / fundraising ring / personal founder rhythm. |
| Moment | Date, time zone, rhythm and decision context. |
| Evidence authority | The Nature Way gate, data, professional input, or policy that retains decision authority. |

## Reflection entry

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Symbolic lens | A concise user-directed interpretation, clearly labelled symbolic. |
| Reflection question | “If this lens is meaningful to you, consider whether…” |
| Known facts | The evidence already available. |
| Assumptions / uncertainty | What is not known and must not be predicted. |
| Grounded action | One reversible action, preparation step, conversation, or observation. |
| Evidence check | What would make the action useful, unnecessary, or unsafe. |
| Boundary | What the reflection must not decide. |
| Review point | A practical date/event to inspect the action and evidence. |

## Daily and weekly cards

### Daily card

```text
Focus: [one founder responsibility]
Reflective prompt: [conditional question]
Grounded action: [one reversible action]
Evidence to check: [metric, conversation, or proof]
Boundary: [what remains an evidence/professional decision]
Close: [what happened, without causal claims]
```

### Weekly card

```text
What progressed in product / customer / operations?
What evidence changed an assumption?
Where did founder energy or attention create risk?
Which reflection was useful, and which was merely interesting?
What one Nature Way or fundraising gate must be advanced next?
What needs rest, delegation, professional input, or an explicit no?
```

## Review rules

Retain only what serves the user’s stated practice. Do not infer causality from coincidence. Do not export birth data, spiritual notes, or personal reflections into investor materials, employment records, customer records, or shared team documents without explicit consent and an independent legitimate purpose.
