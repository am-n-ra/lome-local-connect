# Product-to-Capital Proof Handoff Reference

Use this reference whenever a fundraising claim depends on product, commercial, technical, or operational work produced through Nature Way. The fundraising narrative may compress evidence, but it must not change its status.

## Build a capital-proof packet

| Fundraising claim | Product proof ID | Evidence class | Source / as of | Metric definition | Investor interpretation | Remaining limitation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<claim>` | `<PR-ID>` | `<observed/reproduced/bounded/external/manual/unproven>` | `<source/date>` | `<basis>` | `<what it supports>` | `<gap>` | `<role>` |

Use only `reproduced` or `external` evidence for unqualified investor-facing claims. Label `bounded`, `manual`, and `unproven` evidence explicitly. Never upgrade a product proof classification while translating it into a deck, memo, or spoken pitch.

## Synchronise change across both methods

If the product changes a metric definition, pricing, permissions, data authority, launch geography, product availability, reliability status, or operating owner, update the capital-proof packet, claim-to-source ledger, data room, deck, and investor update before the next external use. If a fundraising commitment changes product priorities, return to Nature Way Seed/Root System and record the impact before building.

## Confidentiality tiers

Mark each item as `public`, `shareable after initial meeting`, `confidential after approved access`, or `internal only`. Apply the stricter product, privacy, customer, and contractual rule when categories conflict. Record who approved disclosure; do not trade customer identity or personal data for fundraising convenience.
