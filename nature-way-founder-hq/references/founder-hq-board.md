## Weekly review

1. Confirm the active milestone and remove work that does not advance it.
2. Check proof quality and residual gaps before discussing presentation or expansion.
3. Reconcile product commitments, capital assumptions, and external opportunity obligations.
4. Inspect capacity: name the work displaced by every active opportunity or process.
5. Record one keep/change/pause/stop decision with an owner and review trigger.
6. End with one next action per owner, not a broad wish list.

## Event-routing checklist

| Event | Immediate route | Required update |
|---|---|---|
| Material product failure or launch guardrail breach | Nature Way | Proof ledger and launch envelope. |
| Customer evidence changes a major assumption | Nature Way | Seed/Root/Trunk and Founder HQ Board. |
| Investor or diligence request | Fundraising | Claim/capital-proof packet and tracker. |
| New grant, partner, or program | Opportunity Intelligence | Source record and qualification card. |
| Financing or opportunity changes product scope | Nature Way + Fundraising/Opportunity | Decision record and affected plans. |
| Personal founder uncertainty | Sidereal Reflection, if requested | Private reflection ledger; evidence authority unchanged. |
