# Omni V2 — Seed Reframe: Local Commerce Search Engine

**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Status:** Founder correction — Species reopened

## Core product proposition

Omni is first a **local commerce search engine**. The Buyer landing is not a dashboard, storefront, or role selector. It is a living globe/map that makes nearby and global local commerce discoverable, with search as the primary interaction. The user opens Omni to answer: “Who has what I need, where, at what Omni price, and can I rely on that information now?”

The map is therefore the product’s front door, not merely a background behind a search form. The globe gives the product its identity and communicates that local supply is being indexed and made visible. Search, filters, availability freshness, facility cards, Bulk Facility and transaction intent unfold from that landing without forcing the user to understand Buyer/Seller/Admin concepts first.

## Experience hierarchy

| Priority | User-facing surface | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Globe/map landing | Immediate discovery, spatial trust, local supply visibility |
| 2 | Search dock | Search a product, service or facility without leaving the map |
| 3 | Search results / map pins | Compare facilities, net price, quantity fit and freshness |
| 4 | Facility/product sheet | Inspect offers, public QR entry and manual/Bulk availability |
| 5 | Intent and transaction | Decide to buy only after availability verification |
| 6 | Seller workspace | Appears only after explicit Seller entry and permission context |
| 7 | Admin workspace | Appears only for explicit staff roles and server-confirmed capabilities |

## Landing behavior

On open, the globe/map loads immediately in the monochrome visual language. A slow globe rotation may be used only in the idle resting state and must stop when the user searches, touches the map, opens a sheet, receives a location prompt, or enters a transaction. The search dock remains the dominant control, while map controls stay quiet and secondary.

The landing must support three low-friction entry points: typing a product or service, browsing the map, and scanning a public facility QR/link. The public QR entry opens the same facility/product context inside Omni but does not become a transaction QR. The product-search path and the facility-QR path converge at the public facility/product sheet, then diverge only when the Buyer chooses availability verification or confirms “Je veux acheter”.

## Role presentation rule

Buyer is the default experience. Seller and Admin are contextual workspaces reachable through authenticated capabilities, not primary landing navigation. No Seller/Admin menu option should be visible unless the server-backed account context grants it. A user who is not a Seller sees a clear but secondary “Devenir vendeur” entry, never Seller operations. Admin and Reviewer tools remain completely absent for ordinary users.

## Species gate reopened

The new Species must first approve the globe/map landing, idle/search states, search results over the map, facility/product sheet, public QR entry, and the transition into intent.
