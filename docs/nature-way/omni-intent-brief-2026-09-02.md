| Field | Current answer |
|---|---|
| Trigger | A person who needs a product/service has no way to know who has it *now*, in the quantity they need, within budget, near them — unless they already know a supplier or stumble on one via word of mouth, Google Maps or a website. Delivery apps show only certain facility types and areas; they are not a representation of the world's supply. (F1) |
| Problem | Supply is not geographically represented, searchable or queryable by the constraints of the person looking. Availability "at instant T" is unknown. Discovery, availability and transaction are disconnected. (F1, MS §1–3, MV1 §1–4) |
| Affected actors and context | **Buyer**: anyone with a phone, mobile-first, initially Lomé field pilot **[inferred from REPO]**. **Seller/Provider**: any supply — formal shop, market stall, individual, mobile professional, digital provider — who should not have to maintain a full inventory. **Operator/Admin**: Omni team + field agents who convert facilities, verify claims and audit. (F1, MV1 §6, §11, §63) |
| Current alternatives | Google Maps/business profiles (static, no availability), delivery apps (narrow supply), word of mouth, phone calls, walking around. Sellers: website or nothing. (F1) |
| Desired outcome | "Make the world's supply visible, searchable, understandable and eventually transact-able." For V1: a buyer finds where something exists near them, learns whether it can satisfy their request now, and converts that into a QR-traced Omni transaction; each completed transaction updates Omni allocated stock so availability gets more reliable. (MV1 §79, §82) |
| Harm or failure to avoid | Claiming precision Omni does not have (fake availability, fabricated supply, "verified" without evidence); requiring sellers to build perfect stores or manage full inventory; exposing seller contact before genuine intent; Omni becoming a payment processor, delivery app, directory or AI-first product in V1; destructive changes to existing Neon identities/records. (MS §10, §148, MV1 §73) |
| Smallest critical journey | Buyer opens the map → searches "X" with optional budget / quantity / radius → sees facilities whose offers match → checks availability at one facility (manual seller reply, or deterministic automatic reply from Omni allocated stock) → "Je veux acheter" → transaction QR issued → seller verifies QR → discounted price applied → external payment declared and confirmed → fulfilment confirmed both sides → rating → Omni stock event. (F1, MV1 §38–42, §79) |
| Success signal | One real Lomé seller and one real buyer (not team) complete the full loop with a QR-traced transaction and a resulting stock event; the seller's allocated stock and "3 verified sales" counter move. Secondary: availability requests answered within freshness window; `qualifying_sales` progression. **[inferred]** |
| Constraints and resources | Solo founder + AI; mobile-first PWA now, native later; French UI (vouvoiement); MapLibre only (no Google Maps); Neon Postgres + Vercel; FedaPay for wallet reloads only; no in-app payment of goods; must preserve existing DB records; limited dev capacity is *why* allocated stock + availability requests exist instead of inventory. (F1, MP §7) |
| Non-goals (V1) | Global multi-facility cart; AI agents; ads; in-app goods payment; full inventory management; social features; global scale; browser extension; OSM mass import beyond seeding the map. (F1, MV1 §65, §73, MS §199) |
| Assumptions / unknowns | A-1 Sellers accept a mandatory discount on every Omni offer because it funds the traceable loop (F1) — medium confidence, review after 5 real sellers. A-2 Buyers will send availability requests rather than call (MV1 §33) — untested. A-3 Deterministic auto-availability from allocated stock is trustworthy enough within a freshness window (F1, MV1 §37) — untested. A-4 The Lomé pilot is the first geography **[inferred]**. A-5 One identity with buyer + seller capabilities (MV1 §11) vs role toggle (MP §3) — decision needed. |
| Risk classification | **Elevated** — handles identity, seller payment details (displayed text), wallet ledger with real money reloads (FedaPay), location data, and production database with existing records. Qualified review required before any migration touching `v2_facilities.trust_state`, wallet ledger, or payment declaration semantics. |
