## 5. Agent base salary / incentives (OPEN)

The 15,000 F/day covers transport + food. Objective is a **credible team**, so base compensation and/or Pro-sale commission must be added — founder to decide (placeholder in scenarios: 5,000 F/day/agent).

## 6. Vehicle option (Hiace) — OPEN, to source

Capex-vs-opex: daily transport for **2×N agents** at 10,000 F/day is:
- N=2 → 4 agents → 880,000 F/month just transport
- N=3 → 6 agents → 1,320,000 F/month
- N=5 → 10 agents → 2,200,000 F/month

A vehicle replaces the transport line but adds fuel/maintenance/insurance/driver. Break-even ≈ (purchase + running) vs transport avoided. **No price assumed by HQ** — local sourcing needed before the specialist models it.

## 9. Honest finding for the founder and the specialist

- At a **focused pilot scale (2 zones, 3 months)**, the honest pre-YC operational need is **≈ 9–10M F — well below HERLOG's 50M floor**.
- The **coverage ambition (Lomé-all in 3 mo + Aflao M3 + team 5–10 + mobile dev + legal) lands at ≈ 15–31M F** on these placeholders — **still under HERLOG's 50M floor**.
- The **≥50M hypothesis therefore does NOT automatically follow** from the founder's ambition. It holds only with an **extended horizon (6 months) and/or heavier lines** (larger dev/mobile budget, vehicle fleet, higher salaries, bigger legal + reserve cushion).
- This is a **founder ambition decision, not a model consequence.** The fundraising specialist must (a) run the ≥50M stress check honestly, (b) weigh bridge size vs HERLOG floor vs YC W27 timing, (c) decide whether the bridge is genuinely ≥50M or whether HERLOG becomes the *next* round post-proof. HQ does not decide.
