# Evidence, Narrative and Claim-Ledger Reference

Use this reference when preparing a pitch deck, investment memo, investor update, model narrative, or diligence response.

## Keep facts distinct from claims

| Content class | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Verified fact | Cite source, as-of date, owner and definition. |
| Derived metric | State formula, source inputs, period and calculation owner. |
| Forecast | Label as forecast; state assumptions, sensitivity and owner. |
| Hypothesis | Label as hypothesis and define the next validation event. |
| Fundraising ask | State amount logic, milestone, time horizon and use-of-funds categories. |

## Claim-to-source ledger

Maintain one row per material statement. Reconcile every repeated figure before distribution.

| Claim ID | Claim | Class | Source / location | As of | Definition / formula | Owner | Status / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CL-001` | `<statement>` | Fact / Derived / Forecast / Hypothesis | `<file or system>` | `<date>` | `<basis>` | `<role>` | `<verified/gap>` |

## Evidence quality rules

Prefer recurring payment, cohort retention, signed and invoiced contracts, renewal, contribution margin, customer interview evidence, and repeatable product use over vanity metrics. Build market size from identifiable customer counts, realistic pricing, and a credible expansion ladder—not population totals or arbitrary market-share claims.

Define revenue, active user, customer, churn, retention, CAC, contribution margin, gross margin, burn and runway before comparing periods. Separate cash collected, booked revenue, gross transaction volume and net revenue. Preserve negative trends with their context; do not rename a weak metric to remove it from the story.

## Narrative spine

Write a concise answer to: why this team, why this customer pain, why now, what has been proven, what remains uncertain, how the market can expand, what capital unlocks, and how the next milestone will be measured. Ensure the deck, memo, model and verbal pitch use the same terms and time periods.
