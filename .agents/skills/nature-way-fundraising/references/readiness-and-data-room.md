# Investment Readiness and Data-Room Reference

Use this reference to diagnose readiness before starting broad investor outreach. Score for verifiable evidence, not confidence or presentation quality.

## Seven-pillar diagnostic

Score each pillar from `0` to `3`: `0` absent, `1` started, `2` mostly present but incomplete, `3` independently verifiable by a stranger. Select the two weakest material pillars as the next remediation cycle. Do not use a total score as a funding guarantee.

| Pillar | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Legal and corporate | Incorporation, cap table, founder agreement and vesting, IP assignment, employment/contractor terms, material licences, taxes/filings. |
| Financial | Management accounts, separated company banking, model assumptions, burn/runway, unit economics and outstanding obligations. |
| Product | Live product or bounded proof, roadmap tied to evidence, codebase ownership, reliability and security responsibilities. |
| Market | Bottom-up, countable market logic, first-customer definition, go-to-market sequence and regional expansion assumptions. |
| Team | Founder-market fit, critical skills, role clarity, hiring plan and credible advisors. |
| Governance | Decision records, board/advisory rhythm, compliance ownership and reporting cadence. |
| Documentation | Permissioned data room, current source files, findability and response-time ownership. |

## Minimum data-room index

| Area | Typical contents | Owner | Freshness / access test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate | Formation documents, cap table, shareholder/founder agreements, IP assignments, prior financing, licences. | `<owner>` | `<date and permission>` |
| Financial | Management accounts, company statements, tax status, model, burn/runway, debt/convertibles. | `<owner>` | `<date and reconciliation>` |
| Commercial | Customer list and revenue basis, contracts, pricing, pipeline, churn/renewal evidence. | `<owner>` | `<date and confidentiality>` |
| Product and team | Demo or sandbox, architecture summary, roadmap, security posture, org chart, employment/contractor terms. | `<owner>` | `<date and role access>` |

Apply a response-window test agreed with the team: if a material item cannot be located, permissioned and explained within that window, mark it as a readiness gap. Do not publish customer-identifying data or personal information wider than authorised.

## Remediation record

For each gap, record the pillar, evidence missing, owner, smallest next action, deadline, risk if unresolved, and verification method. Do not silently mark an item complete because a document exists; check that it is current, internally consistent and accessible to the appropriate reviewer.
