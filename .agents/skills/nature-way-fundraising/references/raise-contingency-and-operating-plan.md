# Raise Contingency and Operating Plan Reference

Use this reference when a company is preparing for, running, pausing, or extending a raise. It protects the operating business from becoming dependent on an unclosed financing process.

## Maintain two tracks

| Track | Required question | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Operating plan | What work, spend, hiring, commitments, and customer obligations continue if no new capital arrives by the planning date? | Default-alive plan and current runway basis. |
| Fundraising plan | What evidence, relationships, meetings, diligence, and closing conditions are being pursued? | Raise tracker and process calendar. |

Do not fund operating commitments solely from expected capital until executed conditions and funds receipt justify the change. Keep the plan factual; this is a governance and risk-control artifact, not personalized financial or legal advice.

## Define process triggers

| Trigger | Required response |
|---|---|
| Evidence gap discovered | Return to the claim, product-proof packet, or readiness remediation owner; do not narrate around it. |
| Pipeline loses fit or momentum | Recheck capital fit, relationship route, and the operating-plan implications before expanding outreach. |
| Runway or material obligation changes | Update the capital thesis, default-alive plan, use-of-funds logic, and authorized decision owners. |
| Term or diligence condition changes the product plan | Open a Nature Way Seed/Root System decision before committing scope or resources. |
| Raise pauses or closes unsuccessfully | Preserve relationships, document learnings, continue the default-alive plan, and schedule the next evidence gate. |

## Protect execution capacity

Set a named operating owner and fundraising owner, a cadence for status review, a maximum meeting/process load the operating team can sustain, and a decision right for pausing outreach. Record material feedback, declines, and diligence gaps in the learning loop with a classified next action: `evidence`, `product`, `narrative`, `capital-fit`, `process`, or `no action`.
