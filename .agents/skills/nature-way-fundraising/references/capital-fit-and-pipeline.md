# Capital Fit and Investor-Pipeline Reference

Use this reference before research, outreach, or relationship mapping. Fit comes before prestige.

## Assess capital fit

Compare each potential capital type against stage, proof, amount, dilution, repayment, reporting, governance and timing. Do not treat names, cheque ranges or headline categories as guarantees of availability.

| Capital type | Typical fit signal | Main obligation or risk |
|---|---|---|
| Grants and competitions | Early experimentation, impact or programme fit. | Reporting, restricted use and programme rules. |
| Angels / accelerators | Early proof, founder support or network need. | Dilution, cohort requirements or individual concentration. |
| Venture equity | Evidence of scalable, fund-returning potential and expansion path. | Dilution, governance, outcome pressure and future-round expectations. |
| Strategic / corporate capital | Clear commercial, distribution or product alignment. | Dependency, conflicts and strategic constraints. |
| Revenue-based finance | Predictable, repeatable receipts. | Revenue share and cash-flow constraints. |
| Debt / asset finance | Assets, receivables or predictable repayment capacity. | Repayment, collateral, covenants, currency and timing risk. |
| DFI / blended finance / family office | Relevant impact, geography or relationship and commercial case. | Mandate, process length, reporting and bespoke terms. |

Escalate legal, tax, securities and debt-structure decisions to qualified professionals. Do not recommend a specific instrument or counterparty as a personal financial decision.

## Build a matched pipeline

Create a pipeline with a named reason for every target. A focused universe is preferable to a large unqualified list.

| Target | Capital type | Stage / mandate | Sector / geography | Likely cheque / capacity | Relationship route | Conflict / exclusions | Evidence fit | Status | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<name>` | `<type>` | `<fit>` | `<fit>` | `<basis>` | `<warm route>` | `<check>` | `<why now>` | `<state>` | `<date/action>` |

Use explicit statuses such as `research`, `relationship`, `intro_requested`, `first_meeting`, `follow_up`, `diligence`, `paused`, `declined`, `closed`, and `not_a_fit`. Record the source and date for mandate information. Do not imply a warm relationship, investment interest, cheque size, or decision timeline without evidence.
