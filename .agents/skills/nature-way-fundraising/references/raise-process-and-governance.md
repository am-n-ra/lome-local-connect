# Raise Process and Governance Reference

Use this reference for a live raise, diligence, term-sheet sequence, closing preparation, or post-close operating rhythm.

## Process map

```text
capital thesis
  → readiness remediation
  → evidence and data room
  → matched relationship pipeline
  → coordinated meetings and follow-ups
  → diligence requests and verification
  → term-sheet comparison and professional review
  → closing conditions and funds confirmation
  → post-close reporting, governance and next milestone
```

Treat each transition as a gate. A meeting is not diligence, a term sheet is not cash received, and funds received are not the end of investor operations.

## Active-process discipline

Maintain a process tracker with contact, confidentiality status, shared materials, requests, owner, response deadline, facts disclosed, next action and known risk. Follow up promptly and truthfully. Coordinate timing without making false scarcity claims. Do not distribute sensitive data before the appropriate confidentiality and permission conditions are satisfied.

## Term-sheet and closing boundary

Record economic terms, governance/control terms, protective provisions, conditions precedent, exclusivity, fees, deadlines, legal counsel, cap-table impact and unresolved questions. Do not present legal, tax, securities or valuation conclusions as settled without qualified professional review. Confirm closing only when executed documents, conditions, funds receipt and ownership records match the agreed transaction.

## Post-close rhythm

Agree a recurring investor update containing performance against the financed milestone, cash/runway, material wins, material risks, asks, governance decisions and next period priorities. Send candid updates even when results are below plan. Preserve a decision log and a source trail so the next fundraising ring starts with credible operating evidence.
