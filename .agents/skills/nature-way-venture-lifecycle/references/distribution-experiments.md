# Distribution Experiment Reference

Use this reference to test how a specific reachable segment discovers, evaluates, activates, and repeats value. It is not a promise of scalable growth.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Target segment and context | Who has the problem and where they can be reached. |
| Channel hypothesis | The single route being tested and why it could fit the segment. |
| Message / offer | The honest promise and relevant call to action. |
| Value event | The behaviour that indicates meaningful value, not merely reach. |
| Measurement basis | Date range, source, cost/capacity, denominator, and known limitations. |
| Failure condition | What would cause the hypothesis to be changed, paused, or stopped. |
| Service constraint | What support, onboarding, fulfilment, or product reliability is needed. |
| Result and learning | Observed outcome, alternative explanation, and next action. |

Run one channel thesis at a time when possible. Do not compare channels with inconsistent offers, audience definitions, time windows, or measurement bases. Escalate privacy, marketing-law, platform-policy, and partner-commitment questions to the appropriate qualified and Nature Way gates.
