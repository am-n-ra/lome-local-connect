# Nature Way Founder Learning

## Resource loading and attestation

Treat every named `references/` or `templates/` resource as a required input when its stated trigger applies. Load the exact file or directory before performing the dependent work, then include a **Resource Receipt** in the phase output: `Loaded`, `Template instantiated`, and `Not loaded / reason`. Do not claim to have followed a resource that was not loaded. If a required resource is unavailable, state the gap and stop before the gate it controls.

For every active capability gap, load [references/intra-skill-execution-controller.md](references/intra-skill-execution-controller.md) and instantiate [templates/intra-skill-plan.md](templates/intra-skill-plan.md). Create the local task tree before assigning practice, feedback, or transfer work, and return its evidence and task changes to Founder HQ.

Treat learning as a venture capability system, not a content-consumption backlog. The correct question is not “what should the founder learn?” It is **“what capability gap is preventing the next evidence-backed milestone, and is learning the right response?”**

## Relationship to the Founder HQ ecosystem

Use **Nature Way Founder HQ** when an active milestone repeatedly stalls, evidence quality is weak, a decision requires unfamiliar expertise, or the team is unsure whether to learn, hire, delegate, use a tool, seek an advisor, or defer. Use this skill to close the capability gap; return the resulting evidence or capacity change to the primary product, lifecycle, fundraising, or opportunity gate.

Sidereal Reflection may help a consenting founder select a personal study rhythm or reflection prompt. It must not determine aptitude, diagnose gaps, screen people, or override the practical learning plan.

## Capability-gap loop

### 1. Detect the gap from work

Start with a concrete venture failure, bottleneck, risk, or missed opportunity: incomplete customer interviews, weak product instrumentation, unreliable delivery, unclear pricing, absent distribution evidence, unprepared diligence response, repeated sales objection, or unowned compliance question. Do not begin from self-judgment or a generic wish to “learn more.”

### 2. Classify the response

Choose one or more of these responses, with a reason:

| Response | Use when |
|---|---|
| Learn and practise | The capability is core to the founder/team’s ongoing work and can be safely developed in time. |
| Hire or contract | The work requires depth, speed, accountability, credentialing, or sustained capacity beyond the current team. |
| Advisor, mentor, or peer review | A focused outside perspective can improve judgment, feedback, or network access. |
| Tool, system, or template | The gap is repeatable process overhead rather than strategic understanding. |
| Delegate | A capable existing owner can hold the work with clear authority and evidence. |
| Defer or decline | The capability is not currently on the critical path or the opportunity is a distraction. |

Do not prescribe learning where legal, clinical, regulated, security-critical, or safety-sensitive work requires qualified professionals.

### 3. Design a practice loop

Define a bounded learning objective tied to a live venture artifact. Select a short input, then require practice, feedback, revision, and evidence. Examples include running and reviewing real customer interviews, instrumenting one value event, producing a source-backed pricing analysis, rehearsing a pitch against a rubric, writing a distribution experiment, or reviewing an operating risk with an expert.

Avoid measuring learning by courses watched, pages read, credentials collected, or time spent alone. Measure whether the venture artifact, judgment, decision quality, or operating result improved.

### 4. Obtain feedback and prove transfer

Use customer, peer, mentor, expert, system, or outcome feedback appropriate to the gap. Record what changed after feedback and whether the improved capability transferred into the active milestone. If it did not, revise the practice design or choose a different response such as hiring or expert support.

If a bounded practice loop fails to transfer twice, do not add more study by default. Reassess the gap, time pressure, feedback quality, and risk; then explicitly switch to hire, delegate, advisor, tool, defer, or a materially different practice design.

### 5. Refresh the capability map

Close, maintain, or reopen the gap. Update the Founder HQ Board with only the capability relevant to the current milestone, owner, chosen response, evidence of transfer, and next review. Keep personal learning notes private unless the founder explicitly chooses to share them.

Read [references/capability-gap-loop.md](references/capability-gap-loop.md) before creating a learning plan, skill-gap assessment, founder development roadmap, or advisor/hiring decision.

## Capability areas

Assess only the areas relevant to the active venture stage: customer discovery and research; product/design; engineering/data/security; distribution/growth; sales/partnerships; pricing and unit economics; fundraising and governance; operations; leadership and hiring; domain/regulatory knowledge; communication and negotiation. Do not turn this list into a personality score or universal founder checklist.

## Operating rules

| Rule | Required behaviour |
|---|---|
| Gap from evidence | Tie the gap to a blocked or risky real venture outcome. |
| Response before curriculum | Decide learn/hire/delegate/advisor/tool/defer before assigning content. |
| Practice over consumption | Produce or improve a live artifact with feedback and revision. |
| Transfer over completion | Close the gap only when it changes venture evidence, quality, speed, or risk. |
| Respect expertise | Escalate regulated, legal, security, health, and safety work to qualified professionals. |
| Capacity protection | Do not add learning work that silently displaces the active milestone. |
| Personal-data boundary | Keep reflective or personal data private and never use it to assess another team member without consent. |

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Capability-gap record | Observable bottleneck, affected milestone, risk, evidence, and owner. |
| Response decision | Why the venture will learn, hire, delegate, seek advice, tool, or defer. |
| Practice plan | Objective, live artifact, feedback source, revision loop, transfer signal, and time/capacity limit. |
| Capability map | Current critical gaps, status, dependencies, evidence of transfer, and next review. |

## Definition of done

Call a learning loop complete only when the gap is tied to real work; the chosen response is explicit; practice produces a live artifact or decision; appropriate feedback was used; transfer is visible in product, customer, distribution, fundraising, or operating evidence; and the Founder HQ Board records the remaining boundary or next review. Never claim that a founder is fully prepared, expert, or guaranteed to succeed because a curriculum was completed.
