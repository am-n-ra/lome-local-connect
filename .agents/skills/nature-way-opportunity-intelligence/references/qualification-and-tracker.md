# Opportunity Qualification and Tracker Reference

Use this reference to decide whether an opportunity deserves founder attention. A score supports comparison; it does not override a veto or professional-review requirement.

## Qualification card

Score each dimension `0` to `3`, where `0` means absent or harmful and `3` means strong and evidenced. Record the reason, not only the number.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Strategic fit | Does it materially advance the active product, commercial, or capital milestone? |
| Explicit eligibility | Can the venture meet every stated requirement now or by a controlled date? |
| Evidence readiness | Can claims, documents, demo, data room, and team proof be supported honestly? |
| Milestone value | Is the likely benefit meaningful relative to the current venture stage? |
| Time and capacity | Can it be pursued without harming the trunk, customer work, revenue, or recovery capacity? |
| Terms and conflicts | Are IP, exclusivity, equity, repayment, reporting, data, publicity, and governance obligations acceptable? |
| Source confidence | Are issuer, availability, deadline, and material terms verified? |

### Veto outcome

Set the card to `decline` or `pause` when any deal-breaking condition exists, regardless of score. Describe the condition and the evidence required to reconsider it.

## Review cadence

At least once per agreed cadence, close expired items, refresh time-sensitive sources, inspect active work-in-progress against the capacity limit, and ask whether each active opportunity still beats the work it displaces. Use `watch` only when an actual trigger or review date is recorded; otherwise decline it.
