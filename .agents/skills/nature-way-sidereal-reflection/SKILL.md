# Nature Way Sidereal Reflection

## Resource loading and attestation

Treat every named `references/` or `templates/` resource as a required input when its stated trigger applies. Load the exact file or directory before performing the dependent work, then include a **Resource Receipt** in the phase output: `Loaded`, `Template instantiated`, and `Not loaded / reason`. Do not claim to have followed a resource that was not loaded. If a required resource is unavailable, state the gap and stop before the gate it controls.

For every requested reflection cycle, load [references/intra-skill-execution-controller.md](references/intra-skill-execution-controller.md) and instantiate [templates/intra-skill-plan.md](templates/intra-skill-plan.md). Use the local task tree only to track consent, reflection, grounded action, evidence check, and closure; it must never assign authoritative product, financial, legal, hiring, health, or safety work.

## Founder HQ integration

Use **Nature Way Founder HQ** when a reflection touches an active venture milestone, opportunity, launch, or fundraising process. Record only a user-chosen grounded action or boundary in the shared board; keep chart data and private reflection material separate. The reflection layer never gains decision authority through Founder HQ.

Use this skill as the **compass beneath the roots**, not as the tree’s authority. It lets a founder explore a Vedic sidereal lens for attention, reflection, personal rhythm, and the questions they may be overlooking. It does not establish what will happen, which investment will succeed, who to hire, when a market will move, or whether a product decision is correct.

## Fixed framework and consent

Use **Vedic sidereal astrology**, **Lahiri ayanamsha**, and **whole-sign houses** by default. State the framework in every reflection. If the user supplies a different calculation or interpretation, treat it as user-provided input; do not silently blend systems.

Request personal birth data only when the user explicitly wants a natal or transit-based reflection. Treat date, time, and place of birth as sensitive personal information: collect the minimum needed, state that time uncertainty can change house/ascendant interpretation, do not infer missing data, and do not persist or reuse it outside the user’s stated purpose. Never analyse another person’s birth data without their permission.

If a chart calculation is needed, use a reputable calculation source chosen by the user or clearly label the chart as user-supplied. Never fabricate positions, divisional charts, dashas, transits, or timing windows.

Set a scope-expiry date for every personal-data use. When the requested reflection or cadence ends, delete or stop reusing birth inputs and private notes unless the user explicitly renews consent for a new stated purpose.

## Authority order

Astrology is a reflective prompt only. Apply this order whenever it intersects a real decision:

| Authority | What it decides |
|---|---|
| Safety, law, finance, medical care, and human rights | Require qualified, evidence-based judgment; astrology has no decision authority. |
| Nature Way Root, Trunk, Heartwood, proof ledger, and launch envelope | Decide product, security, data, release, operational, and customer-facing actions. |
| Nature Way Fundraising capital-proof packet, capital thesis, counsel, and contingency plan | Decide fundraising claims, capital process, disclosure, commitments, and closing actions. |
| Sidereal reflection | Suggest questions, pacing experiments, journaling prompts, and attention themes only. |

When a reflection conflicts with evidence or an approved gate, preserve the evidence-based decision and record the reflection as an optional personal note. Do not use astrology to delay an urgent safety, legal, customer, payroll, security, or contractual obligation.

## Reflection cycle

### 1. Frame the moment

Name the structural path, decision or moment, time horizon, known facts, open assumptions, and relevant Nature Way phase. State whether the matter is reversible, hard to reverse, or high stakes. For high-stakes or irreversible matters, begin with the existing evidence and professional-review path; the sidereal layer may contribute only a short reflection question.

### 2. Establish the chart basis

Record the framework, source of chart data, known input accuracy, date/time zone, and relevant user-selected lens. Separate verified calculation data from the user’s own spiritual interpretation. Do not turn symbolic language into a claim about another person’s motives, future, health, finances, or worth.

### 3. Produce reflective prompts, not predictions

Write a small set of conditional questions in this form: “If this lens is meaningful to you, consider whether…”. Pair every prompt with one observable, reversible action or evidence check. Avoid deterministic language such as “will happen,” “is destined,” “must,” “guarantees,” or “foretells.”

### 4. Counterbalance with reality

For every reflection, name the evidence that must still be checked: customer feedback, financial records, cohort/retention data, legal advice, technical test results, security review, operating constraints, or the appropriate Nature Way gate. Convert only the resulting evidence-backed action into a product or fundraising plan.

### 5. Close the loop

Record what was noticed, what action was taken, the evidence observed, and whether the reflection was useful. Do not treat coincidental outcomes as proof of causality. Revise the personal rhythm based on the founder’s practical wellbeing and operating evidence.

Read [references/reflection-ledger.md](references/reflection-ledger.md) before creating a personal cadence, timing reflection, or decision journal.

## Use time rhythms deliberately

| Rhythm | Purpose | Safe output |
|---|---|---|
| Moment / hour | Pause before a meeting, conflict, or focused task. | One reflection question, one grounded next action, one boundary. |
| Daily | Set intention and inspect energy, attention, and evidence needs. | Daily reflection card; never a prediction. |
| Weekly | Review commitments, founder workload, experiments, and unresolved decisions. | Weekly learning review and a small evidence plan. |
| Monthly | Rebalance product, sales, fundraising, and recovery capacity. | Calendar hypothesis plus measurable operating priorities. |
| Quarterly / annual | Revisit personal themes, venture strategy, and long-range goals. | Reflection letter, decision review, and Nature Way / fundraising gate review. |

Do not claim that a chart supplies an exact hour, day, week, month, or year in which an external outcome will occur. Treat timing as a user-directed prompt for preparation, prioritisation, or recovery.

## Founder and team boundaries

Use personal reflection to support self-awareness, not to rank employees, screen candidates, assign work, judge partnerships, or predict another person’s behaviour. Team decisions require role clarity, consent, performance evidence, and the ordinary Nature Way decision process. Do not ask a team member to reveal birth data or spiritual beliefs.

## Decision handoff

| Situation | Sidereal layer may do | Required Nature Way handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Reversible creative or planning choice | Offer prompts and a short experiment. | Record the experiment and success signal in the relevant slice. |
| Product or customer decision | Surface assumptions or attention themes. | Use Seed/Root/Trunk evidence and customer proof. |
| Launch or migration | Support personal preparation and communication reflection. | Use the launch envelope and proof ledger. |
| Investor meeting or pitch rehearsal | Support grounding, presence, and reflection. | Use pitch-readiness, claim ledger, and capital-proof packet. |
| Financing, hiring, contract, health, or legal decision | Offer no recommendation or forecast. | Escalate to evidence, qualified professionals, and the required decision gate. |

## Required artifact

Create a **Sidereal Reflection Ledger** only when the user wants an ongoing practice. Record the framework, chart basis, consent/scope, reflection prompt, grounded action, evidence check, review date, and what was learned. Keep it separate from authoritative product, personnel, customer, and investor records unless the evidence-backed action itself belongs there.

## Definition of done

Call a reflection complete only when it is explicitly non-deterministic, states its Vedic sidereal Lahiri/whole-sign basis, protects personal data, separates symbolic interpretation from facts, names the evidence-based authority, results in at most a reversible grounded action or journal prompt, and leaves safety, legal, medical, financial, product, and fundraising decisions to their appropriate evidence gates.
